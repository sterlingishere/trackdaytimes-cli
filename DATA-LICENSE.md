## In short

You are free to:

- **Share**: copy and redistribute the data in any medium or format.
- **Adapt**: remix, transform and build upon it, such as charts, analysis, models and research.

As long as you:

- **Credit TrackDayTimes**: say the data comes from TrackDayTimes, link to <https://trackdaytimes.com>, and say if
  you changed it. For example: "Lap data: TrackDayTimes (trackdaytimes.com), CC BY-NC 4.0".
- **Don't use it commercially**: not in anything sold, in a paid product or service, or in advertising, and don't
  sell the data itself. For commercial use, ask for permission first through <https://trackdaytimes.com/support>.

## Also

- Results come from organisers' timing sheets and from TrackDayTimes members. They are provided as they are, without
  any warranty that they are complete or correct.
- Drivers can hide their name on TrackDayTimes. Where they have, the data says "Hidden". Don't try to work out or
  republish who they are.
- API access needs a personal key and can be revoked for misuse, such as sharing a key or getting around the limits.
